PILOT AXIS (Black/White, Premium Minimal)

You are a world-class product designer and conversion-focused brand strategist. Create a premium, ultra-minimalist website for a company called Pilot Axis.

1) Brand & Positioning

Company name: Pilot Axis
Industry: AI automation + AI voice agents for businesses (call handling, scheduling, lead capture, follow-ups, CRM logging, workflows).
What we sell: Done-for-you AI systems that replace missed calls, capture leads, book appointments, manage waitlists, and automate operations.
Target customers: Local service businesses and appointment-based businesses (daycares, clinics, dental, salons, home services, etc.).
Main outcome: More booked appointments, fewer missed calls, less admin work.

Brand vibe:
	•	Premium, minimalist, serious, high-trust, “enterprise-ready”
	•	Not playful, not “startup cute,” not gimmicky AI
	•	Feels like a control system / command center / precision engineering

2) Visual Style (STRICT)

Color palette: ONLY black and white
	•	Background: white
	•	Text: black
	•	Use very subtle gray shades only if needed for dividers (like 5–10% black), but keep it mostly pure B/W
	•	No gradients, no bright colors, no illustrations, no stocky “AI robots”

Typography (choose one pairing):
Option A: Modern premium
	•	Headings: “Söhne” or “Neue Haas Grotesk” or similar clean grotesk
	•	Body: “Inter” or “SF Pro” style

Option B: Editorial premium
	•	Headings: elegant serif like “Canela” or “Tiempos”
	•	Body: clean sans like “Inter”

Type rules:
	•	Large, confident headings
	•	Short lines, lots of whitespace
	•	Tight system: consistent sizes, consistent spacing

Layout rules:
	•	12-column grid, wide margins
	•	Massive whitespace
	•	Minimal borders (thin 1px lines)
	•	Subtle hover effects only (underline, slight shift, opacity)

Imagery rules:
	•	Prefer abstract, monochrome, geometric visuals
	•	Use simple line icons (monochrome)
	•	Avoid busy mockups; if used, keep them black/white and clean

3) Site Goals (Conversion)

Primary CTA: Book a Call
Secondary CTA: Get a Demo or See Use Cases
Lead capture: short form (name, business, email/phone, message)

Trust:
	•	Clear offer
	•	Clear outcomes

	•	Simple process
	•	Clean pricing (optional)

4) Navigation + Header (Sticky)

Create a sticky header with:

Header should be thin, premium, with subtle bottom border line.
